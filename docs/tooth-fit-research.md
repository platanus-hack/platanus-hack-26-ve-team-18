# Tooth and implant fit: implementation research

Checked **2026-10-24** against primary publications, current professional guidance, manufacturer documentation, and the repository's `AGENTS.md` and `skills/dental-restoration-design`. Research only; no patient-specific placement or clinical fit is established. Recommendations marked **engineering** are proposed software behavior, not validated clinical thresholds.

1. **Establish the site before fitting.** FDI 36 denotes the permanent mandibular **left first molar**: quadrant 3 and tooth 6 counted from the midline. ISO 3950:2016 remains the published current edition, with a revision under development. The designation does not establish coordinates, orientation, or whether that tooth is missing. [ISO catalogue](https://www.iso.org/standard/68292.html); [standard §4, printed p. 2](https://cdn.standards.iteh.ai/samples/68292/f72969b198e44f7fbe1caa1347eca0e6/ISO-3950-2016.pdf).

   **Engineering:** for a single missing 36, localize between the reviewed distal surface of 35 and mesial surface of 37, if both are present. Retain a reviewed ridge/surface anchor, source identity, anatomical direction mapping, and source pose. Patient left must survive camera rotation. A nearest unlabeled triangle, broad gap, filename, or screen-left position cannot establish FDI. Multiple missing teeth require explicit slots; equal spacing is only a hypothesis. Do not let a fit optimizer move a crown into a different gap to lower its score.

2. **Use a mandibular first-molar form, then preserve its features.** A useful qualitative prior has mesiobuccal, distobuccal, mesiolingual, distolingual, and a smaller distal cusp; a central fossa and mesial/distal triangular fossae; marginal ridges and separating developmental grooves. The university teaching reference describes greater mesiodistal than buccolingual extent, a narrower distal portion, and unequal cusp heights. These support organized anatomy rather than a symmetric dome with arbitrary bumps. They supply no validated cusp coordinates or groove-depth constants. [Al-Mustaqbal University, Dr. Dhifaf Kamil, Lecture 7, pp. 1–6; undated, 2025 filename](https://uomus.edu.iq/img/lectures21/MUCLecture_2025_6248952.pdf).

   Five cusps are not mandatory patient anatomy: an original 2019 cast study observed four-, five-, and six-cusp first molars, with five most common in its three sampled groups. Its abstract and methods disagree on sample size, so use it only as evidence of variation, not a prevalence estimate. [Bibi et al., 2019, pp. 137–139](https://podj.com.pk/index.php/podj/article/download/373/262).

   **Engineering:** prefer a reviewed suitable donor, including reflected 46 when available, while retaining the donor transform and interpolated areas. A template should be editable. Check the actual exported peaks, ridges, fossae, cervical narrowing, and silhouette after fitting. Avoid enforcing `MD > BL`: an original study of 141 molars in 71 Taiwanese participants found mean MD/BL dimensions of 11.13/10.47 mm without a distolingual root versus 11.00/11.16 mm with one. These are sample means, not recommended crown sizes or evidence for a mesh's scale. [Lee et al., 2016, Table 1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6395370/).

3. **Measure gap occupancy from facing surfaces, separately from clearance.** Official DentalCAD documentation first identifies mesial/distal neighbors and contact points, permits correction of those assignments, and adapts a library tooth. Its freeforming workflow separately supports lateral extension toward adjacent teeth, antagonist adaptation, and basal adaptation for pontics/waxups. It also distinguishes shape-preserving morphing from cutting. These are primary descriptions of CAD behavior, not clinical validation of Platanus. [exocad Place Model Tooth](https://wiki.exocad.com/wiki/index.php/Automatic_Single_Tooth_Placement); [exocad Freeforming, Adapt/Approximal](https://wiki.exocad.com/wiki/index.php/Freeforming) (undated documentation, accessed above).

   **Engineering:** measure available mesiodistal width between opposing neighbor surfaces within a stated contact-height region, not between tooth centroids. Fit the two proximal regions independently. Report mesial gap, distal gap, their locations, crown width, and available width. A dimensionless width/available-width ratio can diagnose underfilling, but must state its measurement plane and has no clinical acceptance cutoff. Preserve embrasures; do not stretch the entire cervical wall to fill every void. Prefer local side deformation and cusp-preserving occlusal adjustment to uniform shrinking. Mark absent neighbors or inadequate segmentation as unmeasured.

   Proximal mesh distance does not measure contact tightness. A 2022 retrospective follow-up classified contact loss using a 0.1 mm cutoff and observed it at 50% of 90 assessed contacts. That cutoff was a study classification, not a target CAD gap. [Gasser et al., methods/results](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/clr.13908).

4. **Fit the crown in a fixed, evidenced bite.** A clinical study of nine fully dentate participants measured repeatability of static interocclusal registration with one scanner/workflow; its digital full-arch result was 31 ± 19 µm. That is precision in that experiment, not trueness, a permissible crown gap, or this case's uncertainty. Buccal bite scans register the arches in the recorded pose. [Morsy and El Kateb, 2022](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12903-022-02612-5).

   **Engineering:** review both bite references and preserve the resulting arch transforms. Bite-reference meshes are registration evidence, not antagonist collision targets. Query the actual opposing upper surface region; do not assume the upper tooth with the same final FDI digit is the sole antagonist. Keep the crown's cervical reference, occlusal height, and abutment height separate. If an intended occlusal contact region is provided, penalize excessive separation there as well as crossing; a one-sided minimum-clearance objective cannot distinguish a plausible crown from one far below the bite. Other cusp slopes/fossae need different relationships, not uniform zero distance everywhere. Without intended contact data, report separation and interference without declaring acceptable occlusion.

   Static geometry does not establish lateral/protrusive clearance, force, or timing. exocad explicitly separates static antagonist adaptation from virtual-articulator movement and dynamic contact visualization. A synthetic sweep should retain that distinction. [exocad contact/occlusion visualization](https://wiki.exocad.com/wiki/index.php/Visualizing_contacts_and_occlusions).

5. **Give depth an explicit datum and keep component interfaces connected.** Distinguish the IOS site/mucosal reference, bone crest, fixture platform, crown base, and occlusal surface. Manufacturer guidance illustrates why these differ: the Straumann BLX manual recommends 0.5–1.0 mm subcrestal placement specifically for healed sites while noting anatomy/restoration dependence. This is a BLX instruction relative to **bone**, not a universal offset from the scanned gum or a default for FDI 36. The indexed manufacturer page 28 was available; full-PDF retrieval exceeded the web reader's size limit, and its revision date was not verified. [BLX manual, p. 28](https://www.straumann.com/content/dam/media-center/straumann/en-us/documents/brochure/USLIT-1205-BLX-Step-by-Step.pdf).

   **Engineering:** in a local frame with occlusal direction `up`, retain the existing explicit construction `platform = siteOrigin - depth*up` and `apex = platform - length*up`. The generic abutment must start at that platform and bridge to the emergence/crown reference. A depth edit should not accidentally drag the occlusal surface below the bite or leave a disconnected abutment. Allow a below-site visual placeholder, labeled hypothesized; do not describe it as subcrestal without a bone-crest measurement. A local surface normal is not automatically a clinical implant axis.

6. **Model emergence as a transition, not one radius or a universal angle limit.** Report platform radius, emergence contour, transition height, and local profile slopes separately. For a coaxial straight-sided diagnostic envelope only, `angle = atan2(abs(rEmergence - rPlatform), transitionHeight)` gives the slope angle to the axis. This mathematical measure is an **engineering** diagnostic, not the measured emergence angle of an arbitrary asymmetric crown. Preserve nonzero transition room when radii differ; zero height produces a ledge, not a smooth emergence profile. Do not automatically deepen an implant to satisfy an angle target.

   The often-cited **30°** value comes from a cross-sectional study: peri-implantitis prevalence in its bone-level group was 31.3% above 30° versus 15.1% at/below 30°; no corresponding association was found in its tissue-level group. This is an association, not proof that every angle below 30° is safe. [Katafuchi et al., online 2017, 2018 issue](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jcpe.12829).

   More recent evidence does not create a universal replacement threshold. A 2025 randomized trial enrolled 47 aesthetic-zone patients and re-examined 42 at three years; convex profiles had more midfacial recession than concave profiles. Its endpoint and site differ from posterior-molar peri-implantitis. [Endres et al., 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12531365/). A 2026 histological study examined 48 implant sites in six dogs at 24 weeks, with 20°/40°/60°/80° abutments and no ongoing oral hygiene; wider angles showed more inflammation. This animal result does not validate a human CAD cutoff at 40°. [Strauss et al., 2026, methods/limitations](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13069363/).

7. **Keep clinical placement and hardware requirements explicit.** The January 2026 ADA/AAOMR recommendations support CBCT for presurgical implant planning/placement (§7.1.1), relevant anatomical relationships (§7.2.2), and three-dimensional bone-volume assessment (§7.2.3). Imaging selection follows clinical evaluation; creating an IOS-only concept does not itself require ordering imaging. [Original recommendations, Box 2, printed pp. 26–27](https://health.ri.gov/sites/g/files/xkgbur1006/files/2026-01/ADA-AAOMR-patient-selection.pdf).

   **Engineering:** a clinically assessed mandibular site needs clinician-reviewed bone/anatomical exclusions, calibrated scale, and registered volumetric/surface data with uncertainty. IOS alone does not expose the inferior alveolar canal, neighboring roots, or lingual bone boundaries. The original AAOMR anatomical discussion identifies the posterior mandibular canal and lingual concavity as relevant constraints; use its anatomy discussion alongside the updated 2026 imaging guidance. [AAOMR, 2012, printed p. 818](https://aaomr.org/common/Uploaded%20files/Position%20Papers/aaomr_implants_position_paper.pdf).

   For an **existing implant**, recover the platform pose through reviewed scanbody registration with the correct implant/abutment-level library. Medit's July 2026 instructions explicitly assign scanbody libraries by tooth and verify aligned scan/library deviation. [Medit Scan Body Library Matching, updated 2026-07-20](https://support.medit.com/hc/en-us/articles/360033527711--Scan-Body-Library-Matching).

   Manufacturing requires the exact connection, compatible component/library version, material, and process. MEDENTiKA's A00 US guide defines gingival, margin, total-design, and post heights separately, with material/connection-dependent thickness and angulation constraints. A generic capped cylinder or crown exterior implements none of those interfaces. [Manufacturer guide, printed pp. 11–15](https://www.straumann.com/content/dam/media-center/medentika/en-us/documents/brochure/UserGuide_708957_A00_US.pdf) (revision A00; date unstated).

8. **Preserve file units and honest measurement labels.** The original PLY specification stores vertex coordinates and extensible properties without a mandatory physical-unit field or scene-transform structure. Neither a PLY extension nor a plausible dental size establishes millimeters. [Greg Turk, PLY specification, 1994, pp. 1–3](https://gamma-web.iacs.umd.edu/POWERPLANT/papers/ply.pdf).

   **Engineering:** retain `file units (u)` and `mmPerUnit = null` until independently calibrated. If verified, convert `distance_u = distance_mm / mmPerUnit` explicitly; keep angles in degrees and ratios dimensionless. A sampled minimum can miss a closer patch. Vertex/sample counts are not contact area; unsigned distance is not penetration depth; open IOS meshes do not supply a reliable solid interior. Report triangle crossings separately from touching/ambiguous intersections, along with sample coverage and incomplete checks. Clinical fit stays unvalidated even when the selected geometry checks pass.

Repository observations before the corrections (read-only research snapshot, 2026-10-24):

- `src/crown-anatomy.ts` already selects a five-cusp hypothesis for a lower digit-6 template. Verify the resulting mesh and anatomical orientation rather than adding another generic molar preset.
- `src/restoration-site.ts` initializes crown width/depth from neighboring-size or fallback ratios (`0.8*scale`, `0.75*scale`) and fixture depth at zero. Those are visual defaults, not measured gap occupancy or bone depth. A poor initial width can survive later bounded fitting.
- `src/restoration-fitting.ts` defaults adjacent/antagonist gaps to `0.015*scale`/`0.025*scale`, uses the closest 8% of adjacent samples, and penalizes only under-gap antagonist samples. These values have no clinical validation in the sources reviewed. Preserve named mesial/distal evidence and add an explicit diagnostic for excessive separation at intended occlusal contacts; do not simply substitute a literature number.
- Its 82% width/depth and 72% height lower bounds prevent unlimited shrinkage relative to the initial shape; they do not prove that initial shape fills the site. Prefer measured surface-based initialization and region-specific deformation. Report infeasible or unmeasured constraints rather than a single unconditional fit pass.
- `src/restoration-geometry.ts` already exposes distinct site/platform/apex/crown-base planes and bridges nonzero fixture depth. Reuse those datums when diagnosing floating components or changing depth controls.

Recommended implementation order: confirm site/frame and source poses; derive facing-neighbor space and antagonist envelope; initialize the correct anatomy; fit named proximal and occlusal regions while preserving morphology; show independent geometry outcomes and remaining clinical unknowns. Keep expensive work in cancellable workers. Geometry/integration changes require the repository's `pnpm test`, `pnpm build`, and browser smoke test; none was run for this documentation-only research task.

## Corrections implemented after the research

- `missing-sites.ts` now measures facing flank-triangle envelopes in a stated local slab and moves an automatic origin toward their midpoint only on its supporting ridge patch. Reviewed anchors are not moved. Vertical measurements use the restorative axis and one site datum.
- `workflow-geometry.ts` sizes the emitted template envelope, including the abutment, against the sampled available headroom. Templates already have cusps, so initial extra relief is zero. Measured width takes precedence over arc-span fallback. Initial 98% envelope occupancy remains an editable engineering heuristic.
- `restoration-fitting.ts` adds forward occlusal-axis ray measurements. The closest 8% of observed hits supply an illustrative approach objective. Accepted candidates must improve score, preserve observed ray-hit coverage, and avoid new tested intersections on previously clear targets. Sampling and bounded intersection limits remain explicit.
- A shared selection rule is used by fitting and session validation. New reports identify their selection policy; legacy score-only histories remain readable.
- Manual edits regenerate and measure exactly the entered dimensions. Optimization is an explicit retry. Fixture depth remains independent of crown position and explicitly referenced to the selected scan surface, not bone crest.
- No physical scale, bone-safe placement, compatible interface, internal crown fit or manufacturing approval is inferred. No dependencies or patient samples were added to the shipped app.
