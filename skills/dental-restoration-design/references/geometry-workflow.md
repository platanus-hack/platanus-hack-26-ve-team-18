# Geometry and evidence workflow

## Intake and coordinate contract

Parse meshes locally with existing validation. Record byte hashes, roles, original bounds, triangle counts, closedness warnings and column-major rigid file-to-workspace matrices. Face labels need a separate geometry/triangle-order hash; filenames replace neither hash. Keep patient files out of source control and raw arrays out of LLM input.

Absent PLY textures do not prevent geometry inspection or establish gingiva/tooth segmentation; connected shells are not clinical labels. Record simplification and use it only for display unless analysis explicitly names the approximation.

Keep upper, lower and bites in one workspace, initially at imported poses. If needed, use local rigid bite refinement against fixed references; retain original/refined poses, transforms and per-reference metrics, and reject failed/partial results. Lower RMS is a fit statistic, not clinical registration accuracy. Registration must not merely eliminate opposing-arch collisions.

Keep `u` and `mmPerUnit: null` until scale is independently verified; record conversion/provenance and explicitly convert clinical/material thresholds. Typical tooth size cannot establish scale. Camera up-axis changes only viewing.

## Establishing a candidate site

Inspect each arch in occlusal, facial/lingual and oblique views, then both in the captured bite. Anchor the hypothetical site to a source triangle where possible; retain local point, face index and provenance. Derive and document the frame from space/neighbouring surfaces. A surface normal alone is not an implant axis.

Keep experimental anatomy hypotheses and supporting views separate from the clinician-entered FDI chart. Identity and location may be independently uncertain; render plausible gap alternatives and select a labeled demonstration hypothesis. A geometry prototype may use null FDI.

Measure available mesiodistal (MD) and buccolingual (BL) space surface-to-surface within stated regions, not between centroids/markers. Measure antagonist headroom from an identified cervical/body datum along the local occlusal axis; name ray, closest-surface or sampling methods and retain misses. Read each target's measured gap band and crossings instead of replacing them with arch averages. Crown dimensions are design values, not available-bone measurements.

## Construction options

Prefer a suitable reviewed donor/template; document segmentation, suitability, reflection/rotation and deformation. Preserve occlusal detail during contact/cervical edits. Mirroring does not establish contralateral symmetry.

Save donor face indices, geometry hash, selection/cut and full donor-to-target transform. Keep rigid source poses separate from donor reflection/scaling/deformation. Correct winding and recompute normals after reflection; record axis scales and inspect distorted cusp relationships. Never silently weld a crop to gingiva or call its cut a reviewed clinical margin. An open visual exterior needs explicit boundaries/omissions and supplies no solid interior or intaglio.

Without a suitable donor, specify procedural cusp centres/heights, footprint, groove depth and cervical taper. The kit supplies topology/coordinate invariants, not validated dental morphology. Inspect the intended tooth form after fitting; an explicitly requested unavailable donor still fails rather than silently using this fallback.

The exterior generator cannot derive roots, intrabony screws, implant connections, intaglio, screw channels or manufacturing minimum thickness. Report unavailable components as unknown, not plausible hardware.

## Geometric audit

Separate adjacent contacts, opposing arch and source-arch/cervical surroundings. Without reviewed segmentation, whole-arch proximity cannot be attributed to a named neighbour. Exclude bite references from collision targets.

Inspect the assembly in its installed source frame: the pilar retention core is expected to nest inside the crown. Same-assembly envelope overlap is distinct from external-target interference, and exploded display separation is not installed clearance. Measure emitted support/enclosure separately from clinical gum emergence; artificial closed envelopes supply no true intaglio, connection or material-thickness assessment. See the [reference-view interpretation](component-parameters.md#reading-the-exocad-reference-views).

Record intersection counts/tolerances and actual sampling: viewport corners differ from fitter triangle centroids. Custom area-weighted/adaptive sampling may improve coverage; never claim it ran without executor evidence. Record source/target resolution, counts, direction, coverage and error bounds; “dense” supplies no bound. Separate fitter crown/pilar checks from `relationships` fixture/pilar-to-component or adjacent-surface samples and signed axial interfaces. Adjacent surfaces are not reviewed roots; construction interfaces are neither bone-crest datums nor validated connections.

Open targets support distances/crossings without a trustworthy solid sign. Reviewed normal-oriented distances are not global signed distances; positive samples can miss closer patches. Any thresholded face-area integration must state threshold, tessellation dependence and approximation, never exact contact area.

Validate finite vertices, indices, winding, edge incidence, degeneracy, component count and export/reimport consistency. Check self-intersection separately or mark untested; manifoldness does not exclude it. Label artificial basal closure in reports/views and retain its collision checks.

Report only the captured static pose. Patient-specific excursions need independent motion data and anatomical kinematics; mesh-centre sweeps are geometric stress scenarios, not jaw simulations. Gaps/intersections establish no occlusal force, pressure, osseointegration or tissue compression.

## Natural crown form from a geometry reference

Inspect the reference from the chewing surface, both sides and the base using neutral shading. Keep observed morphology separate from its filename, material, tooth identity and fit. An STL has no calibrated enamel shade. A CAD attachment, connector pad, thin flap or artificial basal closure is not a tooth feature to reproduce.

For posterior crowns, inspect unequal rounded cusp volumes, the scalloped cusp-to-wall silhouette, broad inward slopes, marginal ridges, branching grooves and localized fossae. Look for connected transitions into softly convex axial walls and a narrower cervical region. A uniform rim surrounding isolated Gaussian bumps, an extruded rounded box or a rigid straight cross can look artificial even with smooth normals. Raising relief or adding texture alone does not demonstrate these features.

Change a bounded procedural shape definition and inspect the actual emitted triangles. Preserve tooth-class and upper/lower distinctions, the saved donor/reference anatomy, topology, source frame and exact requested controls. Use explicit deterministic shape hypotheses; never silently substitute a supplied reference for the patient's crown or copy its dimensions as clinical targets. Remeasure fit after a morphology change because a better silhouette can still introduce a crossing or lose a contact.

Inspect the complete generation path with its relief and contour controls as well as the normalized template. An angularly varying rim can interact with rim-relative scaling and split one intended cusp into two peaks. Verify emitted cusp counts and connected slopes after those transforms; a passing template-only audit does not cover the final crown.

New posterior templates store a shared normalized relief datum at their highest rim. The same increasing height transform then applies across the table and centre, leaving the rim fixed. Retain that datum through editing, adaptation and session persistence. Historical shapes without it retain their recorded per-angle behavior; do not silently change their construction.

## Numeric evaluation contract

Use this contract for reporting; it is not a claim that the current host serializes every field. Read actual tool results first and keep unsupported fields unknown. Preserve evaluator statuses and limits rather than deriving a stronger conclusion from one number.

| Field | Required meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Identity/domain | Assembly, component, metric and separate root/neighbour ID; display, geometry, anatomy or clinical domain. |
| Actual | Finite current measurement or `null`, never a missing-as-zero value. Distinguish emitted extents, controls, samples and manual observations. |
| Target | Bound/equality, comparator, source and applicability; absent is `null`. Prototype tolerances are engineering choices, never implicit clinical defaults. |
| Error | Signed bound violation in compatible units/datums/methods, otherwise `null`; distinct from uncertainty. |
| Units/datum | `u` until verified conversion; degrees or dimensionless ratios as appropriate. Name frame, surface/landmark, axis, region and direction. |
| Provenance | Actual and target each measured, clinician-entered, hypothesized or unknown; source hashes, segmentation/landmark review, poses and assembly/evidence revision. Keep identifiers/geometry local. |
| Method/evidence | Algorithm, sampled/exact-on-mesh scope, coverage, resolution, uncertainty/bounds, numerical tolerance, supporting views/reports and missing evidence. Screenshots supply no calibration. |
| Status/action | Supported check `pass`, demonstrated violation `fail`, unresolved comparison `unknown`; smallest supported edit or missing prerequisite. |

For inclusive `[L, U]`, error is `actual - L` below L, `actual - U` above U, otherwise `0`; absent sides are unbounded. For the confirmed lower bounds use `min(0, actual - 1.5)` **only for a valid mm comparison**. Clearance above the lower bound is margin, not error to minimize. Exact targets use `actual - target` with explicit tolerance. Exclusive boundaries may fail with zero error. Never round 1.49 to 1.5 or invent clinical tolerance to pass.

Verified scale `s` mm/u gives `actualMm = actualU × s`, `targetU = targetMm / s`; require finite positive `s`, provenance, uncertainty and compatible registration. Without it, 1.6 u versus 1.5 mm yields no error/pass/fail: keep the file-unit observation separate and clinical actual/error null. A recorded scale alone does not enable conversion in the current evaluator.

Keep uncertainty separate from tolerance. A sampled minimum to complete targets is generally an **upper bound on the true mesh minimum**; unmeasured source regions can be closer. Above-threshold samples cannot prove clearance without coverage/error bounds; intersections or a valid below-threshold witness can demonstrate a problem subject to uncertainty. Unknown acquisition/segmentation/registration error is not zero; threshold-straddling ranges remain unresolved. Even complete mesh distance is no surgical accuracy claim. See [ITI accuracy consensus](https://academy.iti.org/academy/consensus-database/consensus-statement/-/consensus/accuracy-of-static-computer-aided-implant-surgery/1820) [S6].

Project emitted vertices into the site frame. Nominal dimensions, world-axis bounds and top above site are different quantities with different targets. Neighbour means are engineering references, not clinical size prescriptions; class/cusp counts do not prove anatomy, nor color/roughness shade or physical finish.

### Case placement datums

These targets come from the user's explicit case confirmation, not a literature default. Record them for the selected assembly without re-confirming the wording. The measurement method and anatomical evidence may still be missing.

| Confirmed constraint | Definition needed for measurement | Invalid substitutes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Implant **platform ≥ 1.5 mm below reviewed bone crest** | Identify real platform, reviewed crest region/landmark/plane and apicocoronal direction in one calibrated registered frame. For axial depth, `d = (pPlatform - pCrest) · aApical`, positive apically, needs reviewed point correspondence. Axial projection, plane distance and crest sectors are different methods. Preserve reviewed scalloped-crest sectors and their controlling rule; never choose a favorable point or silently average. | Gingiva, construction `origin`, crown base, apex, unsigned gap or `fixture.depth = 1.5`. Generic fixture top is only a hypothesized platform. |
| Implant **outer surface ≥ 1.5 mm from each adjacent root** | Measure minimum 3D separation between full relevant implant exterior and each identified root in one frame; state method/intersection/containment limits. Cover implant length and every relevant multirooted-neighbour root. Distinguish manufacturer geometry from a generic envelope; retain each result and controlling minimum. | Center/axis distance, crown contact, arch/gingiva proximity, one coronal slice, averaged mesial/distal gaps or fitter crown/pilar samples. |

Aggregate root clearance needs complete relevant coverage: any failure fails, otherwise any unknown root keeps it unknown. Check intersections and, for valid solids, containment; nonintersecting boundaries can hide a contained solid. Never invent an interior for open scans.

Retain targets and list missing landmarks/segmentations, platform identity, scale, registration, coverage, method or executable support even with null actual/error. Never infer roots from crowns or crest from gingiva. Current guardrails return unknown placement; CBCT, FDI selection or confirmed feedback does not enable clinical evaluation. Reviewed manual measurements retain separate provenance, not app verification. Lower bounds do not authorize indefinite depth or a narrower implant without other case/system constraints.

### Mouth-fit goal contract

The worker runs `fitRestorationToGoals`; each trial constructs one design and audits every supplied target. Local initialization and explicit **Fit to mouth requirements** allow coordinated revisions. Exact manual/agent edits and original AI `generationDesign` requests force **`maxCandidates: 1`**, yielding `measurement-only` with requested parameters retained. AI generation also preserves its explicit anatomy source and contour; unavailable donors fail. Already authorized bounded follow-up can use live tools to propose a new measured repair without another fit request or clinician confirmation; each edit remains exact and cannot silently change the original design. A local multi-candidate fit is a separate operation. Clinical assessment still needs its actual evidence.

Local `fit.goal` (`restoration-goal/v1`) contains `before`, selected `after`, `contract`, `targetGaps`, `bounds`, `evidenceRevision`, `attempts`, `selectedAttempt`, budgets/timing, status/termination, emergence and limits. Inputs are cloned before yielding. Initial per-target gaps and absolute requirement bounds stay frozen; growing a crown cannot relax them. Separate search bounds constrain crown width/depth/height, abutment radius/emergenceRadius/height/angleX/angleY, fixture depth and cervicalScale. Read their returned ranges as geometric limits, not clinical prescriptions.

Retry passes the previous report as `goalContract`, reusing its `contract`, target gaps and search bounds. Session loading remaps `targetGaps` keys and hashed target-row IDs throughout before/after/contract/attempt history and emergence observations, so bounds follow their targets. Remeasure current evidence and refresh the fingerprint; changed aliases or a historical `evidenceRevision` do not authorize relaxed targets. Expanded crops add newly measured requirements without rebasing existing rows. A missing prior target rejects the retry before commit. Do not claim arbitrary renamed targets are matched automatically. Rebuilding without the contract explicitly establishes a new basis. Agent edits inherit the current goal contract; manual exact edits currently establish a fresh measurement contract, so retain the original task baseline for cumulative review.

| Priority | Current goal rows and interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| `required` | Each target's crossings; each adjacent target's sampled proximal gap band; each antagonist's forward-ray coverage and gap band; component-presentation checks; independent `component-support.pilar-enclosure`; fixture–gingiva exposure and separate emitted pilar/gum checks when present. Missing adjacent/antagonist targets produce unknown rows. One passing contact cannot stand for another. |
| `preference` | Neighbour-relative emitted width/depth/top and anatomy proportions/anisotropy. These guide shape, not mandatory clinical dimensions, and cannot compensate for required failures. |
| `clinical` | The confirmed platform-to-crest and fixture-exterior-to-each-root lower bounds remain **1.5 mm**, with actual/error null; clinical pilar/gum emergence remains unknown independently of engineering support. IOS gum, adjacent crown gaps and file units cannot resolve these rows. |

Small spaces mean close **facing contact regions** within the returned file-unit bands, not zero distance everywhere or all-surface contact. Coordinate supported crown width/depth/height/rotation/contour and pilar radius/emergenceRadius/height/angleX/angleY against measured MD/BL space, headroom and support. Reducing width to clear one target can lose the other contact; raising the seat can consume headroom; increasing a pilar radius can lose crown enclosure. Remeasure every target after each proposal. The automatic goal loop searches only its returned bounds; broader manual/agent controls do not add search dimensions to that loop.

The **`component-support`** quality criterion measures every distinct emitted post vertex strictly above `abutment.height`, without a gum prerequisite. Its feedback row **`component-support.pilar-enclosure`**, labeled **Pilar support vertices outside crown**, reports the outside-sample count. Read `supportSamples`, `enclosedSamples`, `outsideSamples`, `unknownSamples`, `sampledMinInternalClearance`, `sampledMaxOutsideDistance`, `numericTolerance`, `seatingDatum` and `supportTop` from actual criterion evidence. Zero outside samples with unknown tests is not a pass; legacy geometry has no post and remains unknown. These are sampled enclosure/internal-clearance observations, not continuous enclosure, wall thickness, retention strength, contact area or overlap volume. The readonly construction profile cannot be edited; use a fresh construction comparison to evaluate nested support against legacy.

Visible tissue targets use **crown-body** crossing geometry; inspect the constructed collar separately. Unclassified source-arch obstacles still use crown plus pilar. The transgingival pilar intentionally bridges the gum boundary: local `goal.emergence` reports its separate per-tissue-target transverse/touching-or-ambiguous counts and audit completeness, without requiring zero crossings or claiming a validated emergence contour. Read the emitted pilar-support report/required row when supplied: unsupported above-gum samples, unknown support tests and missing gum rays are engineering evidence, not clinical tissue validation. A legacy report without that audit does not prove support.

Fixture–gingiva evidence uses the emitted fixture vertices, including rings and cap centres, with opposing rays along the site axis against all supplied tissue triangles. A pass needs every sample known, no misses/ambiguous layers and no degeneracy; numerical/size limits cannot become a pass by truncating coverage. A witnessed exposure can fail even with incomplete coverage; absent exposure with missing rays stays unknown. Only complete usable evidence can propose a bounded depth increase; preserve the frame and crown position, regenerate the bridging pilar and remeasure. This is a directional visible-gum envelope, not continuous surface coverage, 3D containment, bone depth, root clearance or clinical safety. **Exception to frozen row bounds:** gingiva numerical allowances are recomputed from emitted Float32 coordinates and incidence, with per-sample decisions. Preserve its evaluator status; comparing maximum exposure with the displayed maximum allowance alone cannot establish pass.

For small residual proximal gaps, `planProximalAdaptation` can propose independent local wall-band variants. Emitted displacement is bounded by **3% of the proposal input crown width**; cervical basal ring, rim/rimZ, occlusal/apex geometry, component parameters, frame and pose remain unchanged. Its provenance marker prevents stacking another adaptation, including after parameter edits; later parameter changes rescale the patch and require fresh audit. This is a proposal, not acceptance or anatomy reconstruction. Regenerate and fully audit **all targets**, including both neighbours, antagonist, crown-body tissue and other obstacles, before goal admission. Retain self-intersection/manufacturing limits.

The loop allows at most **13 measured attempts including attempt 0**, with a shared deadline and cancellation; failed construction/audit trials consume entries too. Read the returned budget, since callers may tighten it. It proposes coordinated width/height/depth/cervical changes, pilar adjustments or evidence-supported generic fixture depth. Local admission preserves target identities/roles/sides/gaps/component scopes, complete audits and gingiva coverage, available required measurements, required passes and existing antagonist hits; crossings cannot increase or unknown required rows become failures. It then needs lower aggregate `requiredError`, or equal required error with lower `preferenceError`. This is not a guarantee that every already-failed row improves; inspect all rows. The agent edit gate below additionally prevents each required numeric deficit worsening.

Read `goal.after` and `selectedAttempt`, not the last rejected trial. Only current `status: 'satisfied'` supports meeting recorded requirements within their limits. `unmet` means a required/clinical failure; `unknown` retains unresolved non-preference evidence. A search round with no required failure stops `needs-evidence` when unknowns remain. Current clinical placement and gum-emergence rows prevent overall satisfaction even when engineering pilar support passes. Preserve returned termination at budget boundaries: `candidate-budget`, `time-budget` and `stalled` retain the best admissible result, not proof of success or impossibility; `measurement-only` records exact construction. Initial measurement timeout errors. Zero error, lower score, unchanged/generated geometry or improved appearance cannot establish success.

The UI shows required/preference/clinical groups, before/after bounds/errors, failures/unknowns, search bounds, attempt history and separate pilar evidence; stale reports are historical. Chat exposes assembly-level `goal`, a numeric projection: before/after rows, selected attempt, status/termination, bounds and attempt summaries. It omits the local contract, target map, evidence fingerprint, full attempt snapshots/reasons and emergence report. Goal rows retain `exclusiveUpper`; equality at a strict upper bound fails even when the arithmetic error is zero. Read the preserved status and matching `quality.feedbackRows` when available. Do not invent omitted fields or a chat fitting tool.

### Manual and agent edit loop

1. **Read the baseline.** Get the selected assembly, fresh revision, spec, emitted dimensions, measured MD/BL space/headroom, `goal`, every `quality.feedbackRows` target/band/crossing, `quality.failureFeedback`, criteria, support/emergence relationships, guardrails and views. Keep original task constraints for cumulative review. Goal-backed comparisons reuse the contract above; legacy comparisons hold pre-edit row bounds fixed for each comparison. Candidate-dependent tolerances cannot excuse a larger error. Deliberate target/evidence changes need a new comparison basis.
2. **Choose a supported edit.** Name component/domain, reason, old/new values, expected effect and affected checks. Coordinate the bounded dimensions, rotation, contour and pilar controls above when the measurements require it; do not shrink all surfaces to repair one crossing. A class edit may replace donor anatomy with a procedural hypothesis; inspect it and preserve reviewed tooth identity separately. Neither manual entry nor a class label confirms anatomy or clinical appropriateness.
3. **Keep budgets separate.** `MAX_ASSISTANT_EDITS` remains **8 requested edits per user question**, including rejected/failed requests; views do not reset it. Read `editsRemaining`, host runtime/cancellation limits and tighter task bounds. The local 13-attempt goal budget does not grant more agent edits. Set a deadline for any independent loop; schema ranges are not clinical edit ranges.
4. **Stage and await.** Read `getRestorationDesign` before `requestRestorationEdit({ assemblyId, expectedRevision, edit })`. Submit one exact candidate, then await the host; `requested` / `applied: false` is pending, only `status: 'applied'` confirms commit. `status: 'error', code: 'rejected'` retains geometry/history and returns staged `rejectedCandidate.rows`, `changes`, `failureFeedback` and optional relationship evidence. Rejection is **before commit**, not rollback. Refresh inventory after either outcome and use its current token, even if unchanged; count every retry.
5. **Review every target.** Compare before/after actuals, bounds, signed errors, statuses, feedback, relationships and views with compatible units/datums/methods. Separate rejected values from the retained model. Recheck dimensions, both facing contacts, antagonist headroom/gaps/crossings, cervical contour, pilar support/emergence evidence, integrity and anatomy. Respect strict comparators: equality may fail with zero arithmetic error. Missing values, ray misses or capped crossing tests never prove clearance; display edits cannot improve clinical status.
6. **Preserve evidence.** Agent admission needs current independent evaluation and a measured baseline. With goals, it checks required rows and clinical placement guardrails, preserving passes, measurements, target/hit sets, complete crossing/gingiva evidence and nonincreasing required absolute errors against pre-edit bounds within numerical tolerance. Component relationships must retain measurements/complete audits without increased crossings. Legacy comparisons use numeric feedback/guardrails. Preferences are not required rows. Respond to a concrete rejection reason with a different measured candidate within budget. Host rejection memory applies within one question to the exact assembly/revision/evidence; do not repeat the same rejected patch there. Changed evidence or revision clears that memory, not the eight-request budget. Valid completed manual edits bypass the admission gate and retain visible failures; validation, stale evidence, worker errors and cancellation still prevent application. Review regressions and use a supported restore/edit route with remeasurement when appropriate.
7. **Stop and report.** Stop at supported objectives, missing dependent evidence/capability, no admissible improvement, repeated lack of progress, or budget/deadline. Refresh after stale/busy/invalid/failed/cancelled work before retrying. Report current actual/target/error/units/datum/revision, applied changes, separately labeled rejected evidence and unresolved checks. Missing bone/root evidence calls for evidence, not an invented fixture edit; independent authorized cosmetic/geometric work continues without re-confirmation. Engineering passes never confer clinical approval.

Feedback rows derive existing measurements; they neither measure geometry nor convert units. Applied results may include `changes`, `failureFeedback` and `relationshipChanges`; rejected measurements stay under `rejectedCandidate`. Transport deltas are not admission tests: changed bounds yield unknown trends/null error deltas while the gate compares pre-edit bounds. Admission need not lower score or fix existing failures; an appearance edit may preserve evidence. Relationship distances/axial interface gaps have no clinical acceptance bound or monotonic-improvement rule; they cannot establish manufactured seating, bone depth or root clearance. Preserve limits and absent values.

## Iteration artifacts and evidence

Use explicit objective regions and clinician-configured contact/clearance targets. Without clinical targets/scale, optimize only a labeled prototype envelope. Separate intended contact from penetration; preserve tooth morphology instead of indiscriminate shrinking.

Bound runtime/iterations; snapshot hashes, matrices, site state, annotation revision and parameters at dispatch. Cancel stale work and recheck every dependency before applying. Keep the best candidate and failures; save spec, mesh, audit, reproducible commands, occlusal/oblique views and both neighbour/antagonist close-ups. Deliver unresolved bite/site/fit uncertainty with the actual concept, never a false pass.
